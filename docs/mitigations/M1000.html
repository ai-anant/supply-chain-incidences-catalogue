M1000

Limit Publicly Available Information

Be cautious about what information is publicly available, including on websites, social media, and other public sources, as it can provide valuable clues to attackers about naming conventions or other system details.

Applies to

  • T0101 Discover technology stacks
  • T0100 Discover naming conventions
  • T0106 Scan configuration on public resources
