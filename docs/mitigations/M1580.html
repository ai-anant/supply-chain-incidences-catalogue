M1580

Restrict access to templates

Storing the templates in a secure location can help prevent unauthorized access to the templates.
This can include encrypting the templates and restricting access to them to only authorized personnel.
Restrict access for templates modification for authorized persons only.

Applies to

  • T0158 Vulnerable CI/CD template
