AS320 · 2016-08

WoSign and StartCom issued unauthorized certificates and backdated SHA-1 certs

Certificates / trust stores

Mozilla's CA:WoSign Issues page documents a series of failures at WoSign
and its acquired StartCom: the 31 August 2016 discovery that WoSign had
issued certificates for GitHub without GitHub's request or authorization
(via a WoSign employee-controlled StartSSL account after StartCom's
acquisition); issuance of 1,132 long-lived SHA-1 certificates between
January and March 2015, with 76 of them backdated to before 16 June 2016
to evade the SHA-1 sunset; undisclosed acquisition; and inconsistent
disclosures. Google, Mozilla, Apple, and Microsoft progressively
distrusted WoSign and StartCom roots and intermediates; certificates
from them stopped being accepted in mainstream browsers in 2017.
