AS60 · 2026-08

Firefox Add-ons crypto-wallet theft

Browser extensions

Socket documented 77 malicious Firefox Add-ons impersonating crypto
wallets and Web3 helpers. Once enabled they intercepted wallet
interactions in the page, stole seeds and private keys, and forwarded
them to attacker infrastructure. Companion campaign to the Chrome/Edge
drainers (AS58), on Mozilla’s store.
