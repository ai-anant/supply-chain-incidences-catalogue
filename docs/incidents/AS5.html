AS5 · 2017-09

CCleaner 5.33 signed ShadowPad backdoor

Windows software

In September 2017 Avast/Piriform shipped CCleaner 5.33.6162 with a
ShadowPad-family backdoor compiled into the legitimate binary and signed
with Piriform’s real code-signing certificate. Avast’s investigation
found the initial access was an unattended developer workstation reached
over TeamViewer; more than 40 machines and the build system were later
infected. Tens of millions of downloads occurred before the poisoned
version was replaced. Talos and Avast published the technical follow-up.
