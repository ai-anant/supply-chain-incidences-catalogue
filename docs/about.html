About

What this catalogue is

Incidents, the platforms they hit, conference talks, the sources those came from, and the technique matrix those incidents are mapped onto.

How it is maintained

Most of the updates are handled by automated processes. A scheduled job polls the tracked research feeds daily, maps new write-ups onto techniques, adds the incident records and rebuilds this site. A second job retries if a run fails or the catalogue goes more than a day without a change. Human review happens on top of that — the guidance reviews, corrections and structural changes are made by a person, not generated unattended.

Every entry cites its primary source. Where a record is thin, or the source does not support a mapping, the gap is stated on the page rather than filled.
