SCIC / Techniques / T0215

T0215 · Defense Evasion · TA07 · observed in 1 incident(s)

Enroll attacker-owned code-signing identity

Artifact Security Open Source Security Secrets Hygiene

The attacker registers or purchases their own legitimate code-signing
identity — an Apple Developer ID, a Windows Authenticode certificate, or
a marketplace publisher account — and signs malware with it. macOS
notarization and Gatekeeper then pass with no warning dialog, and
signature-based allowlists see a valid, unrevoked signature. Distinct
from T0208 (Compromise code-signing identity): there the cryptographic
identity belongs to the vendor and was stolen or misissued; here the
identity is genuinely the attacker's, obtained through the normal
enrollment process. That means vendor-side certificate hygiene cannot
prevent it — only teardown of the certificate after the fact, and
provenance checks on the distribution channel, apply.
