SCIC / Guidance

Guidance

Defender documents (OWASP, NIST SSDF, SLSA, CISA) describe controls. This catalogue describes attacker techniques. This page records where a control had no matching technique, and what we added.

Why a technique can exist with zero incidents. Some techniques come from pentest findings and published research, the same way other catalogues list a technique before a public victim write-up exists. Dim cells on the matrix are identified from literature (24 right now). Bright cells are observed in a mapped incident. That is not a claim the unused ones are fake. We have not yet attached a named public case.
