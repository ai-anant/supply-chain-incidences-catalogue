AS82 · 2026-03

Neutralinojs org force-push (DPRK / PolinRider)

Git / GitHubnpm

On 2 March 2026 at 04:38–04:40 UTC compromised contributor alphagamer7
force-pushed four Neutralinojs repos (neutralinojs, neutralino.js,
neutralinojs-cli, github.io) in 132 seconds. Commits were backdated
5–35 days and attributed to maintainer shalithasuranga or
github-actions[bot]; GitHub triggering_actor showed the real pusher.
Payloads hid after hundreds of spaces in JS configs, used BeaverTail,
and pointed at Vercel C2. OSM tied it to PolinRider and the same
obfuscation family in 106+ other repos. OSM opened a cleanup PR;
Vercel took down the C2.
