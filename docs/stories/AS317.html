AS317 · 2013-12

ANSSI intermediate used to intercept Google SSL traffic

Certificates / trust stores

In December 2013 Google's certificate pinning detected a certificate for
*.google.com issued by an ANSSI subordinate CA. The French network
security agency (ANSSI) had delegated an intermediate to TRECAN, a
private company, whose employees ran it inside their IREM network
appliance — a man-in-the-middle proxy — instead of an HSM. In that
configuration the appliance could and did mint certificates for arbitrary
domains; the observed use was Google traffic interception inside the
network. Google's 12 December 2013 post says eight certificates were
issued for seven domains, none observed in the public internet outside
the affected network. ANSSI restricted the intermediate to government
and French public institutions pending investigation. Google's earlier
MCS Holdings write-up describes this as the same failure mode repeated.
